## 2. What Blesser does

Blesser is a research assistant for people applying to graduate programmes. It finds programmes and faculty, reads professors' public publications, ranks how well each professor's research fits the interests you describe, and drafts first emails for you to review. Gradis, the assistant, answers questions about your search and helps with your documents.

Results are produced by automated tools and AI models from public sources, so please keep in mind:
- **They can be wrong, incomplete or out of date.** A professor may have moved, stopped taking students or changed research direction. Check anything important, such as deadlines, requirements and funding, with the university before you rely on it.
- **Fit scores are estimates** based on published work. They are not a judgement of you or of the professor, and they do not predict admission.
- **We do not guarantee any outcome**, including replies from professors, interviews, offers or admission.
- **Blesser is independent** and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any university or professor it lists.
- **Blesser does not give** legal, immigration, financial or professional admissions advice.
