# Logos

The Sunrise: a half sun rising behind three steps, mirror-symmetric about its vertical axis. The steps are the path an application climbs; the sun is the opportunity coming into view. The sun's base meets the middle step's outer corners exactly, so nothing pokes out at the sides.

- `blesser-color.svg`: sun in `sun`, steps in `forest`, no container. The logo in every product header, sidebar and footer, beside the wordmark.
- `blesser-mark.svg`: the same drawing on a rounded forest tile with white steps. Only where the mark stands alone at icon size: favicon, extension and app icons, share images, email avatar.
- `blesser-symbol.svg`: one-colour forest, for single-ink print and watermarks. A gap separates sun from steps so the shapes do not merge.
- `blesser-symbol-white.svg`: the one-colour version in white, for forest bands and dark photography.

On dark grounds (dark mode, forest bands) the colour logo keeps its sun and turns the steps white.

Wordmark: "blesser" in lowercase Figtree 800, `-0.04em`, `forest` (white on dark grounds), set to the right of the logo with a `space-1` (4px) gap, cap height roughly the steps' height. Never put the wordmark in a container, never recolour the sun, never rotate or skew the mark. Minimum size 16px; clear space a quarter of the mark's width.
