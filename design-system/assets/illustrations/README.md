Blesser makes opportunities (grants, fellowships, scholarships, accelerators) visible to startups, researchers and students in Ghana, and helps them track applications to a yes. Illustrations appear on the public website (large scenes) and in the member app (small spot illustrations for empty states and moments).

## Style: The look (all of these, every image)

- **Ink line + flat shape.** Confident hand-drawn black ink outlines (#14241c), slightly irregular like a brush pen, medium weight, no sketchy hatching, no gradients, no texture, no shading, no drop shadows.
- **Figures:** people of Ghana and West Africa, shown with dignity. Skin is a single flat deep-brown or near-black fill (#3b2316 or #14241c) with no internal facial line work beyond a simple natural profile; faces mostly in profile or three-quarter with minimal features (no detailed eyes, no exaggerated lips, noses or hair; never caricature). Natural hair, braids, head wraps, fades. Contemporary everyday clothes: shirts, trousers, dresses, some with simple wax-print-inspired blocks (no kente copies). Clothing is mostly white/unfilled, defined by the ink line, with ONE or TWO garments or objects filled in an accent colour.
- **Palette (flat fills only):**
  - sun #ffbe1a: the big sun disc, accent objects (a folder, a letter, a scarf)
  - bloom #ff7d66: small celebration accents only (a ribbon, an envelope, a sleeve)
  - forest #0f4f3b and a lighter leaf tone #6f9c82: plants, banana/plantain leaves as flat tonal silhouettes
  - ink #14241c, white #ffffff
  - backgrounds: either flat #f6f7f1, flat sun-soft #fff3cc, flat leaf-soft #e1f1e8, or transparent
- **Signature motifs (use where they fit, not all at once):** a large flat sun disc behind the main figure; tropical leaves (banana, plantain, palm) as flat silhouettes in forest tones at the edges; rounded steps or a path rising toward the sun (echoes the logo).
- **Composition:** lots of breathing room, one clear focal figure or a small group, simple ground line. Movement and optimism: people walking forward, reaching, looking up, working together, celebrating. Never sad, never lonely, never passive.
- **Never:** text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI screenshots, phones showing apps, flags, kente patterns, Black Star symbols, lightbulbs, rockets, graduation caps thrown in the air, money/cash piles, caricature features, servants or service roles as the subject.
