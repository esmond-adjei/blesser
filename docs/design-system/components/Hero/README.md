# Hero

The landing page's first screen: one promise in the serif, one sentence of how, two actions, and the product film.

On the site: `website/src/components/sections/Hero.astro` with `FilmPlayer.astro`. A centred section with `.type-eyebrow` (gold-ink, sentence case), `.type-hero` (one italic phrase for the turn), `.type-lead` in `label-secondary` (max 672px), Start your search (`g-btn--primary g-btn--xl`) and Try it without signing up (`g-btn--plain g-btn--xl` with an arrow), then the product film: one run from a question to an approved email, 16:9, with a round play and pause button on `material`.

Copy rules: the headline is a benefit in under ten words; the lead says what the product does in one sentence that ends on the person's control ("You decide what gets sent"). No stats, no logos wall, no gradient glow behind the headline.

Top padding is 80px, 112px from 640px; the headline drops to 56px under 640px and 44px under 480px.
